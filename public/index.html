Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Without one you can upload 5 files a day, and each stays up for up to 24 hours.

Does the other person need an app?

No. They tap the link and watch in their browser.

How big can a file be?

Up to 64MB without an account. Sign up for larger files.

Can I add a password?

Yes. Set one before uploading and the page asks for it.

Can people download the file?

Anyone can watch. Downloading takes a free account.
